About & privacy

Save Your Dua is a local prototype. Personal duas and Curhat notes are stored in your browser using localStorage. They do not sync to another device. Community features are not connected to a server.

Your saved content may be lost if browser data is cleared or the browser is uninstalled. Make a backup first, just in case.
